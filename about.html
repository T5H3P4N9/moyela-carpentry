Our Story

Built on Passion, Driven by Quality

Moyela Carpentry was founded by Sphiwe Mokoena over 10 years ago with a simple mission: to deliver dependable, high-quality carpentry to homeowners across Gauteng.

What started as a one-man operation has grown into a trusted name in kitchen units, wardrobes, and custom woodwork. As a registered company, we take pride in every project — treating each home as if it were our own.

We don't do ceiling installations — we focus exclusively on what we do best: kitchens, wardrobes, and bespoke woodwork, ensuring every piece is crafted to perfection.
